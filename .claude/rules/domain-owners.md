# Domain Owners

The roster of domain owners: for each owned subsystem, the person who answers for it under Article 3.
Rules refer to "the domain owner" and cite this file; they do not repeat a handle.

## Roster

- **STEBBS** (`suews_phys_stebbs.f95`, the STEBBS data model, the STEBBS fixtures): `@yiqing1021`.

## Changing the roster

- The maintainer adds, removes or reassigns an owner, in its own pull request.
- When an owner changes, keep the previous owner in the entry with "until <date>", so that an earlier record naming the domain owner and a date still resolves to one person.
