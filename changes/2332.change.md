- [change][experimental] Remove deprecated `supy.validation` module, `ValidationController`, `ValidationResult`, `ValidatedConfig`, legacy feedback helpers, and legacy `precheck_*` / `run_precheck` / `SeasonCheck` YAML helpers in favour of `supy.validate_config` and `ValidationEngine` (#2332, #2308)
  - Migrate validation-only callers of `supy.validation.validate_suews_config_conditional(...)` / `ValidationController(...).validate_config(...)` to `supy.validate_config(source, pipeline="ABC", mode="public", forcing="on", science_fixes="suggest")` returning a structured `ValidationReport` (check `report.is_valid`, `report.errors`, `report.warnings`, `report.to_dict()`, `report.to_text()`)
  - Migrate `run_precheck(...)` / `precheck_*` callers: use `supy.validate_config(source, pipeline="B", ...)` or `suews validate --dry-run` for read-only reporting, or `supy.validate_config(path, pipeline="ABC", science_fixes="apply", write_outputs=True)` (checking `report.is_valid` and reading the published `updated_<name>.yml`) for file-based preprocessing; `validate_config` returns a `ValidationReport` rather than a transformed dictionary directly
