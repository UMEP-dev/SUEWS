- [change][experimental] Checkpoints move to schema version 4: the restart envelope's timer keeps only `dt_since_start` and `tstep`, and version-3 checkpoints are rejected with a message to re-run the preceding segment. The kernel timer (`SUEWS_TIMER`) now carries only the eight caller-supplied time inputs; decimal time, weekday, daylight saving, day-boundary predicates, right-closed accumulated-day identity, and local-standard irrigation delivery day are derived every step in `SUEWS_CLOCK` instead of mutating a day latch in the timer (#2333, #2379)
